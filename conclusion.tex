\subsection{Results}
From figure \ref{fig:sunrise} there is a noticeable dip that appears in the middle of the year.
This is due to the rolling back an hour that is done around the United States for daylight savings.
The average sunrise time for each month except for June in 2024 was above 6am.
For June the average sunrise was at 5:55am \ref{fig:averages}.
For the entire year the average sunrise was at 6:41am.
These numbers were noticably effected by the daylight savings time change that is done twice a year by rolling the clock back one hour, then back forward at a later point in the year.
